## 🧭 How I build

> **Design for the phone in the pocket.** If it works on a feature phone over a weak signal, it works everywhere.
>
> **Make it feel alive.** Motion, depth and detail are how people know someone cared.
>
> **Put the rules where they can't be skipped.** Constraints belong in the system, not in a reminder.
>
> **Ship, then sharpen.** Real users teach faster than perfect plans.
